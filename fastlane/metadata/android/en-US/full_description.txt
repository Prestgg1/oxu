<b>Oxu</b> turns books, documents and web pages into calm, plain text you can read and translate.

<p><b>How it works</b></p>
<ol>
<li>Open a file or a web page, its text appears on screen</li>
<li>Long-press to select the sentence you want</li>
<li>Tap &quot;Translate&quot; and read it in your own language</li>
</ol>

<p><b>What you get</b></p>
<ul>
<li>PDF, EPUB, DOCX, ODT, FB2, HTML and plain text files</li>
<li>Web reader: open a link, or share it from your browser, and read only the article text, without menus, ads or pictures. Pages you read stay available offline</li>
<li>Text shared from any app opens straight in the reader</li>
<li>Offline translation on your phone with Mozilla's Firefox Translations models: download the languages you need, nothing leaves the device</li>
<li>21 languages, including English, Turkish, Azerbaijani, Russian, German, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese. You pick both the language of the text and the language to translate into</li>
<li>Adjustable text size and a dark theme, no account, no ads, no tracking</li>
<li>Recent files and pages that remember where you stopped</li>
<li>Three translation backends: offline (on device), MyMemory (an online service, free, no API key, daily limit) or LibreTranslate (your own server, no limits). Online services can fall back to downloaded languages when there is no connection</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>Oxu has no analytics. Reading works entirely offline. Network requests are
only made for what you ask for: the translation of the text you selected (sent to
the online service you chose in Settings, or nowhere with offline translation),
downloading the offline language list and models from Mozilla, and loading a web
page you opened.</p>

<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<p>Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. Scanned or image-only PDFs contain no text to
extract, so they are not supported.</p>
